Frequently asked questions

I bought the unlock but levels are locked again.

Open any locked level, then tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Google or Apple account you used for the purchase.

I changed phones. Is my progress lost?

Your progress is stored on your device. If your phone's system backup is on (Google backup or iCloud backup), it comes back when you reinstall the game. Your unlock is never lost: use Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store, so refunds go through them: request one from your Google Play order history or from Apple's "Report a Problem" page.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Our games work fully offline and never ask for an account.
