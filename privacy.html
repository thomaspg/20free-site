20free — Privacy Policy

Our games collect no data. None.

This policy applies to all mobile games published by ANATHOPEE GESTION under the 20free brand.

  • No account. Our games require no registration, no email, no login.
  • No data collection. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or device data. There are no analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs collecting anything.
  • No ads. Our games contain no advertising of any kind.
  • Offline. Your game progress is stored on your device. The only network request our games make is to display the list of other 20free games.
  • Backup. Your device's operating system may include your game progress in its automatic backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS), so it can be restored if you reinstall the game or switch phones. This is a system feature tied to your own Google or Apple account: the backup is encrypted, we never receive or access it, and you can turn it off at any time in your device's backup settings.
  • Purchases. The one-time unlock purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see or store your payment details. Refer to your store's privacy policy for the transaction itself.

Questions? contact@20free.eu — or see our support page.
